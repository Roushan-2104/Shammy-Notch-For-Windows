# Creative references: 3D and craft for product sites (16 Nov 2026)

These were browsed in the built-in browser. Pinterest asked for a sign-in, so it gave only a preview: glass spheres and glowing orbs on dark. Each reference names one technique to take; none of their assets are used.

## Awwwards, Sites of the Year
- **Lando Norris (OFF+BRAND):**
  - **What it does:** one hero 3D object (the helmet) alone on black, which turns as you move.
  - **Take:** the island as a real object that turns with the cursor.
- **Igloo Inc (abeto):**
  - **What it does:** an object built from blocks in a 3D landscape, assembled as you scroll.
  - **Take:** the island assembling from its moments as you scroll.
- **Messenger (abeto):** a tiny explorable world you enter.
  - **Take:** the playable desktop as a small world.
- **Opal webcam:** a calm grey page where a hand holds the product and one line says what it is ("A new species of webcam").
  - **Take:** calm confidence; one object, one line.
- **Lusion:** realtime 3D as the brand.

## Awwwards, 3D category
- **Autopilot (OFF+BRAND):** product UI panels float in 3D depth over a landscape.
  - **Take:** Shammy's moments floating at different depths around the island.
- **Recurziv:** a laptop on a rock in a moody scene.
  - **Take:** the PC as the stage.

## Dribbble
- **"Focus your time":**
  - **What it does:** huge type split around one sculpted 3D object, with real UI chips orbiting it.
  - **Take:** the headline splits around the island; feature chips orbit.
- **Dynamic Island shots:** bold live-activity cards (flight, timer) at poster size.

## Cosmos ("3d website design")
- **ONYX:** a chrome object with a prism light leak streaking across black.
  - **Take:** the prism beam behind the glass island.
- **Glass and particle tori, a moss torus:** one strange hero form per page.
- **Glass spheres on dark** (also seen on Pinterest).

## Typography (16 Nov 2026)
- **Typewolf trend:** an editorial serif over a clean grotesk. Examples: Editorial Old with Neue Montreal, Signifier with Switzer, Tobias with Diatype, Arizona Flare with Dia.
- **Fonts the reference sites actually load:**
  - Raycast: Inter and Geist Mono.
  - Linear: Inter Variable.
  - Nothing: Geist, plus NType82 and Ndot (dot-matrix).
  - Arc: Exposure VAR (a serif) and Marlin.
  - Opal: Die Grotesk C.
  - Lando Norris: Mona Sans Variable and Brier.
  - Igloo: text drawn in canvas.
- **Pairings offered** (all SIL Open Font License, each with a Devanagari partner):
  - A, Precise: Geist with Geist Mono, and Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi.
  - B, Editorial: Instrument Serif with Inter, and Tiro Devanagari Hindi for Hindi.
  - C, Wide and bold: Mona Sans (width axis), and Anek Devanagari for Hindi, which has a width axis too.
